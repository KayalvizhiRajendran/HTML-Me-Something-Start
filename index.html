Trip to Statue of Liberty

Posted by Kayalvizhi Rajendran

Entirely the authors personal experience and not intentional if it hurts anyones feeling or views.

One of my most cherished places I visited this summer is our trip to New York City. The apple on top is the visit of Statue of Liberty. It was one of the land mark that teaches us the importance of the word "Liberty" . In this fast forward life, we all need to pause and think of the days when our ancestors begged for a life with Liberty. We are in a world now which has it all plenty and we should be thankful for it always and forever.

We went in a beautiful ferry ride to the goat island where the magestic statue of liberty welcomed us. It was indeed a beautiful and everlasting memory of the look of it. Me and my family enjoyed it together. we will always cherish it. The museum inside gives us a whole lot of information on how this was designed and delivered to US as a gift from France. Indeed a breathtaking view to see up close from the bottom of the status!

Loved each and every minute we spent in goat island seeing the majestic Statue of liberty...
